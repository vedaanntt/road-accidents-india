| 1 | The Kaggle dataset "Road Accident Severity in India" is very likely **not Indian**. Its 12,316 rows and 32 columns match the well-known Addis Ababa (Ethiopia) police accident dataset. Vehicle sizes are in quintals ("Lorry 41-100Q"), there are categories like "Ridden horse" and "Stationwagen", only 177 of 12,316 rows are motorcycles (in India two-wheelers are the biggest group of road deaths), and there is no state column. | `data/raw/kaggle_rejected/Road.csv` | Checked the column values before using the file | Rejected. | 2 | **2023 values are about 3 times too large** in the NCRB cases, road type, time and month tables (14.1 lakh road accidents in 2023, against 4.5 lakh in 2022 and 4.7 lakh in 2024). Only tiny UTs with under 300 accidents a year jump around, and they are noisy in every year (see #12). | SOLVED |
| 3 | **Missing state-years**: Ladakh has no rows for 2018 and 2019, and Andaman and Nicobar Islands has no rows for 2019. | All NCRB files | Script lists every state-year with no row | Ladakh became a separate UT only in October 2019, so before that its accidents are counted inside Jammu and Kashmir.
